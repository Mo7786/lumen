Policies

Shipping & returns

Clear rules before you buy: Standard $6.95 and Faster $12.95 on every order, and a 14-day return window for unused tools in original condition.

Shipping

Two options at checkout (same for every product):

  • Standard (10–25 days): $6.95 on every order.
  • Faster (~6–12 days): $12.95 upgrade — quicker lane when available (covers faster supplier shipping + a small buffer).

We aim to process orders within 1–2 business days. Tracking is sent when the carrier provides a number (via Shopify / your fulfillment app). Delivery times are estimates — customs and local post can add days. We do not offer 1–3 day delivery from overseas warehouses.

Packaging: Your order arrives safely wrapped for transit — not in a LUMEN-branded box. LUMEN is the store name; the tool in the photos is what you receive.

Returns & refunds

14-day returns from delivery — clear rules so there are fewer surprises.

  • Change of mind: Unused item in original condition within 14 days. Email hello@lumenlab.shop with your order number first. You pay return postage unless we agree otherwise. Original outbound shipping is non-refundable.
  • Not eligible: Wear and tear, misuse, damage after delivery, or used / not resaleable items (except faulty — below).
  • Faulty / wrong / not as described: Full refund or replacement. Contact us with photos + order number within 14 days of delivery. We cover return postage for approved faulty cases (or may refund without return for low-value items).
  • Hygiene: Opened personal-care tools (stamps, razors, and similar) may get exchange or store credit instead of a full cash refund.
  • Late delivery: Estimates are not guarantees. We help with tracking; refunds for delay are case-by-case if a parcel is lost or extremely overdue.
  • Refunds: Approved refunds go to the original payment method after we confirm (and after inspection if a return is required). Shopify/card processing fees are not always recoverable on our side.
