Tool care

How to use and care for the tools.

Short guidance for stone, silicone, and LED tools. Follow your device manual for timing and charging. Beauty accessories — not medical devices.

Stone tools (gua sha / rollers)

Always use with a slip (oil or cream). Clean with mild soap and water, dry fully, and store away from hard drops.

Ice rollers & globes

Chill as directed. Wipe dry after use. Do not use on broken or irritated skin.

Silicone brushes & pads

Rinse after every cleanse and air-dry. Replace if the surface cracks or holds residue.

LED masks & wands

Start with short sessions. Keep eyes comfortable; follow the included guide. Charge with the supplied cable only.

Makeup tools

Wash sponges regularly. Replace brow blades and lash pads when worn for safer use.

Hygiene

Do not share hydra stamps, derma tools, or face tools. Sanitize before and after use, follow the included instructions, and keep tools for personal cosmetic use only.
