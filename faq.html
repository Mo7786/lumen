FAQ

What does LUMEN sell?

Glow tools and devices only — LED masks, stone rollers, silicone cleansing tools, makeup tools, and kits. We do not sell stand-alone skincare formulas. Some devices (like the electric gua sha) may include a small supplier oil or accessory in the box — that is not a LUMEN-branded oil line.

Do products come in LUMEN packaging?

No. Orders arrive safely wrapped for shipping — not in a LUMEN-branded box. LUMEN is the store name. The tool matches the listing photos.

Are these medical devices?

No. LUMEN tools are beauty accessories for at-home routines. Follow each product’s use notes and the included manual. We do not claim to treat medical conditions.

How do I clean stone and silicone tools?

Wash with mild soap and water, dry fully, and store clean. See the Tool care page for LED, ice tools, and hydra stamp hygiene notes.

Where do you ship?

Two options at checkout: Standard (10–25 days) for $6.95; Faster (~6–12 days) for $12.95. See Shipping & returns.
