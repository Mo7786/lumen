Routine

A simple morning tool stack

Cleanse with silicone, cool with an ice roller, then finish makeup with a lighted compact. Keep it under five minutes.

Stone

Gua sha and roller without tugging

Always use a slip (oil or cream). Work upward and outward, rinse the stone after, and store dry.

Using an LED mask at home

Follow the device instructions for session length and cleaning. LED masks are cosmetic accessories, not medical treatments.
